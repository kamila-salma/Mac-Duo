## ✨ What is Mac-Duo?

Mac-Duo is a small, clever application that lives quietly in your MacBook's menu bar (the top bar of your screen). It brings that famous "iPhone Duo" flip effect — you know, when you flip your phone over to switch between two apps or screens? — right to your computer. Now you can quickly flip between your active windows or apps with just a simple click or keyboard shortcut. It feels magical, like you're using a phone but on a much bigger screen.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting & FAQ

### "I downloaded it but can't find the app."**
Check your Downloads folder. You may need to double-click the downloaded file to extract the app from its package. Once extracted, drag the Mac-Duo app into your "Applications" folder for easy access later.



### "The app won't open — says it's from an unidentified developer."**
This is standard macOS security. Right-click (or Control-click) the app icon and select "Open" from the menu. Then click "Open" in the pop-up window. You only need to do this once.



### "I don't see the menu bar icon."**
Make sure Mac-Duo is actually running. Look in your "Applications" folder, double-click Mac-Duo, and then check the top-right corner of your screen. The icon is small — look for a tiny flip arrow symbol. If you still don't see it, restart your MacBook and try again.



### "Can I use this on a Windows PC?"**
Mac-Duo is designed exclusively for macOS. It works beautifully on any MacBook or iMac running macOS Catalina (10.15) or newer. For Windows users, unfortunately this specific tool isn't available — but check out similar utilities like "Windows Flip" built into Windows or third-party apps like "AltDrag" for window management needs.



### "Is Mac-Duo free?"**

### "Will it slow down my computer?"**
